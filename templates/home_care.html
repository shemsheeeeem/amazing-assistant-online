FAQs

Common questions

Can't find what you need? Create an account and look around. Posting and applying are free.

What does Amazing Assistant Online actually do?

It connects clients who need help with freelancers who want work. Clients post jobs, freelancers apply with a letter and a rate, and clients choose who to hire.

Who is this for?

Small businesses, studios, and busy professionals who need virtual assistants, designers, writers, developers, and more. And freelancers looking for clear, well-paid work.

I'm looking for work. How do I get hired?

Create a free freelancer profile with your skills and rate, then apply to any open job with a short letter. Clients see your profile and reviews, and you get an update on your dashboard when they shortlist or hire you.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Creating a profile and applying to jobs is free, and you keep the rate you agree on with the client.

How do I choose between applicants?

Open the job from your dashboard to read each letter, rate, and rating. Mark people as shortlisted, hired, or declined. They see the update on their side.

How do reviews work?

Once a client marks someone as hired, both sides can leave one review with a 1 to 5 star rating and a short note. Reviews appear on each profile.

Can I pay in pesos?

Yes. Every job can be priced in US dollars or Philippine pesos, as hourly, monthly, or fixed-price work.
