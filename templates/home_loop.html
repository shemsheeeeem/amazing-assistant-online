Frequently asked questions

What is Amazing Assistant Online?

A job board for hiring and getting hired. Clients post jobs, freelancers apply with a letter and a rate, and clients pick who gets the work.

Does it cost anything to post or apply?

No. Posting a job and applying to one are both free.

How do I choose between applicants?

Open the job from your dashboard to read each letter and rate. Mark people as shortlisted, hired, or declined, and they see the update on their side.

Can I pay by the hour, by the month, or per project?

Yes. Every job is hourly, monthly, or fixed price, in US dollars or Philippine pesos.

How do reviews work?

Once a client marks someone as hired, both sides can leave one review with a 1 to 5 star rating and a short note. Reviews show on each person's profile with their average rating.

Can one account both hire and apply?

Each account is either a client or a freelancer. If you do both, create a second account with a different email.

What happens when I've hired someone?

Close the job from your dashboard so it leaves the board. You can reopen it later if you need more help.
